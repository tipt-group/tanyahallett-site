Proprietary Frameworks

The TIPT Framework Collection™

Three evidence-informed frameworks that turn trauma-informed practice and psychological safety into practical, teachable models for leaders and teams. More frameworks are in development.

Bring This to Your Organisation
Framework 01

TIPT Third Space Framework™

Making hidden psychosocial risks visible: six pillars — safety, trust, connection, regulation, collaboration and growth — for creating a psychologically safer space for exploration and dialogue, operationalised through LEGO® Serious Play®.

Framework 02

TIPT Trauma-Informed Framework™

Understanding trauma. Creating safety. Enabling wellbeing. A human-centred approach to practice and leadership, built on six pillars — safety, trust, choice, collaboration, empowerment and cultural safety.

Framework 03

TIPT CARE Framework™

A trauma-informed approach to conversations that build trust, reduce harm and support better outcomes — Connect, Ask, Respond, Empower, End Well.
