Speaking

Keynotes, workshops, and training

Every session is adapted to the room, but built on the same evidence base: trauma-informed practice, applied to how teams actually work.

Keynote Topics

Signature talks

Each topic runs from 45 minutes to a full day, and is tailored to your sector, audience seniority, and desired outcome.

Flagship

The Nervous System at Work

How stress physiology shapes decision-making, conflict, and culture under pressure, and what leaders can do, structurally, to change it. Ideal for opening or closing keynote slots.

Flagship

Psychological Safety After Rupture

Why repair, not the absence of conflict, is the real marker of a psychologically safe team, and how to build repair into everyday leadership practice.

Leadership

Leading Without Re-Traumatising

A practical brief for leaders navigating change, restructure, and disclosure: how to hold difficult conversations without compounding existing harm.

Model

The Grounded Trust Framework: A New Model for Team Safety

A deep-dive keynote or workshop introducing Tanya's proprietary four-pillar framework, with room-specific application exercises.

Policy

From Compliance to Care: Trauma-Informed Policy in Practice

For HR, people, and culture leaders: how to move trauma-informed language out of policy documents and into lived practice.

Custom

Bespoke Sessions

Every talk is adapted to your context. Bring a specific challenge, a restructure, a culture reset, a leadership offsite, and Tanya will build the session around it.
