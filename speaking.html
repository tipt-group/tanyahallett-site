Interactive Keynote · 45–60 Minutes

Leading Psychological Safety in Times of Change

Creating teams that stay connected, adaptable and resilient when everything else is changing.

In today's workplace, change is no longer an event, it's the environment. Restructures, uncertainty, technological disruption, workforce shortages and increasing psychosocial demands mean leaders are expected to guide their teams through constant change while maintaining trust and performance.

This highly interactive keynote combines the power of LEGO® Serious Play® with trauma-informed leadership principles to help participants explore what psychological safety really looks like during periods of uncertainty.

Rather than simply hearing about psychological safety, participants build, reflect and discuss the leadership behaviours that help people feel safe to contribute, innovate and adapt.

Participants will discover

  • Why people resist change (and why it isn't simply resistance)
  • The hidden impact uncertainty has on performance and decision making
  • Practical leadership behaviours that increase trust during change
  • How psychological safety supports innovation, adaptability and wellbeing
  • How to create conversations people actually want to participate in

Perfect for

  • Leadership Conferences
  • Government
  • Mining
  • Finance
  • Healthcare
  • Corporate Conferences
  • Organisational Change Events

Interactive Keynote · 45–60 Minutes

Building Psychological Safety Brick by Brick

Why psychologically safe teams outperform everyone else.

High-performing teams aren't built through trust falls or team-building days, they're built through everyday conversations.

This engaging keynote explores the science behind psychological safety while participants experience it firsthand through an interactive LEGO® Serious Play® activity.

Participants discover why some teams openly share ideas, admit mistakes and solve problems together while others stay silent, avoid conflict and simply "go through the motions."

Participants will learn

  • The five building blocks of psychologically safe teams
  • Why people remain silent even when risks are obvious
  • How leaders unintentionally shut down conversations
  • Practical ways to increase participation and collaboration
  • Small leadership behaviours that create lasting cultural change

Everyone leaves with their own visual model representing the team culture they want to create.

Perfect for

  • Leadership Events
  • Team Conferences
  • Safety Conferences
  • HR Conferences
  • Learning & Development Events
  • Strategy Days

Interactive Keynote · 45–90 Minutes

Discover Your Superpower

Because not all superheroes wear capes.

Every workplace is filled with extraordinary people, but many don't recognise the unique strengths they bring to their teams.

This uplifting and interactive keynote combines LEGO® Serious Play®, storytelling and practical reflection to help participants discover their individual strengths, appreciate the strengths of others, and understand how diverse talents create high-performing, connected teams.

Participants don't simply hear about strengths, they build them, explore them and leave with a renewed sense of confidence, purpose and belonging.

Whether you're leading a team, supporting colleagues or navigating your own career, this keynote reminds us that everyone has something valuable to contribute.

Participants will discover

  • ⭐ Their unique strengths and "superpowers"
  • 🧠 Why we often underestimate our own capabilities
  • 💜 How confidence grows through recognising our strengths
  • 🤝 Why diverse strengths create stronger, more innovative teams
  • 🚀 Practical ways to use their strengths every day
  • 🌱 How recognising strengths builds resilience, wellbeing and engagement

Interactive experience

Through engaging LEGO® Serious Play® activities, participants will:

  • Build a model representing their unique superpower.
  • Reflect on the experiences that shaped their strengths.
  • Discover how different strengths complement one another.
  • Explore the barriers that prevent people from recognising their potential.
  • Develop a personal action plan to intentionally use their strengths in work and life.

Perfect for

  • Leadership Conferences
  • Staff Conferences
  • Team Development Days
  • Women's Leadership Events
  • Government
  • Healthcare
  • Education
  • Financial Services
  • Community Organisations
  • Employee Wellbeing Events

Participants leave with

  • Greater confidence in themselves
  • A renewed sense of purpose
  • Practical strategies to use their strengths every day
  • Stronger appreciation of the people around them
  • Increased motivation and engagement

Perfect when you want a fun, motivational and highly interactive keynote. Participants don't sit back and listen, they actively reflect, build, share and connect. Every person leaves with a tangible reminder of their strengths and a renewed belief in the difference they can make.

Interactive Keynote · 45–60 Minutes

Leading in Modern Times

The leadership skills nobody taught us.

Today's leaders aren't just managing performance.

They're navigating mental health, psychosocial hazards, remote teams, constant change, emotional labour, conflict and increasing complexity, all while trying to maintain high-performing teams.

This engaging keynote challenges traditional leadership thinking and introduces a modern, trauma-informed approach that builds trust, resilience and psychologically safe workplaces.

Using interactive LEGO® Serious Play® activities, participants explore the capabilities modern leaders need to thrive.

Participants will discover

  • Why yesterday's leadership styles no longer work
  • The modern capabilities employees expect from leaders
  • Leading through complexity rather than control
  • Building trust during uncertainty
  • Practical strategies leaders can use immediately

Perfect for

  • Leadership Conferences
  • Executive Forums
  • Organisational Development Events

Framework Keynote · 45–60 Minutes

What Happens After "R U OK?"

Turning a conversation into meaningful support using the TIPT CARE Framework™.

⏳ R U OK? Day is 10 September — only 1 session remains before then. Enquire now to secure the date →

Asking "Are you OK?" is important.

Knowing what to do next is where confidence often disappears.

This practical and interactive keynote introduces the TIPT CARE Framework™, providing leaders and colleagues with a simple, memorable approach for responding with confidence when someone shares they are struggling.

Participants experience the framework through discussion, reflection and LEGO® Serious Play® activities that build empathy, confidence and practical communication skills.

Participants will learn

  • Why many people avoid mental health conversations
  • What helps, and what accidentally causes harm
  • The four steps of the TIPT CARE Framework™
  • How to listen without trying to fix
  • Knowing your role, your boundaries and appropriate referral pathways

Perfect for

  • R U OK? Day
  • Mental Health Month
  • Leadership Conferences
  • Workplace Wellbeing Events
